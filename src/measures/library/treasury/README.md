### Data

* [The Gross Domestic Product (GDP) deflators at market prices, and money GDP](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/gdp-deflators-at-market-prices-and-money-gdp)

<br>

The GDP deflator of a year $y$, i.e., $\lambda_{y}$, is derived from the previous year's value $\lambda_{y - 1}$, via the equation

$$\lambda_{y} = \Biggl( 1 + \frac{\Delta_{y - 1}}{100} \Biggr) \lambda_{y - 1}$$

wherein $\Delta_{y - 1}$ denotes the percentage change vis-à-vis the previous year; for current and future years, $\Delta_{y - 1}$ is initially an estimate.
